# Paragraph notes and translation

Preserve the proven learning logic of `te-weekly-json-study-notes`.

## Intro note
For a complete article, write concise Chinese `summary_zh`, `structure_zh`, and `writing_features_zh` after reading the full article. Do not fabricate a conclusion the article does not contain.

## Paragraph function
First form the article's macro argument/narrative internally. Each `paragraph_function_zh` must combine the paragraph's macro progression role with a distinctive content anchor. Preserve a distinctive opening device when useful. Do not automatically label the last paragraph as `收束`. After drafting, read only the ordered cues and revise vague/interchangeable items.

Normally use 10-30 Chinese characters in the body, maximum 36 excluding the final `。`; at most one Chinese comma; optional colon; exactly one final full stop; one line; no semicolon/question/exclamation/quotes/emoji; do not begin with `本段` or `作者`; avoid vague labels such as `承上启下`.

## Translation
Translate title, subtitle, headings, and body with whole-article context. Prefer faithful learning-oriented translation with natural Simplified Chinese syntax. Preserve hedging, contrast, cause-effect, and emphasis. For each paragraph: understand and translate the paragraph as a whole first, then segment/alignment into `sentences[]`. Do not independently translate isolated sentences.
